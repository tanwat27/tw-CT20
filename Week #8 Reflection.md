# Week #8 Reflection

## class work

This week I focused on the in class projects and my third big project for the class. For what we were doing in class, I really liked that we were working with audio/visual components, and I'm really glad we got to go over it. I found a cool little animation that Jack Stauber made many years ago of a little guy waking up and another little guy crawling out of him. I wondered what audio would work well with it, and I remembered one of my favorite LCD Soundsystem songs, "Never as Tired as When I'm Waking Up". I thought it worked well both with the overall vibe of the animation, and with the literal waking of the guy pictured. I had a lot of trouble getting the audio to work when I used it as a .opus file, spent a long time trying to figure out if I created a bug or something, and came up with nothing each time. Then I decided to try using it as a .mp3 file, and everything worked perfectly fine. That was frstrating, but I guess maybe there was something wrong with the .opus file or maybe P5js was just being picky. Either way I was stoked to see it all come together. I also had a little bit of fun in the HTML section and treated it a lot less seriously than the professor, i.e. referring to LCD Soundsystem as "Mr. Soundsystem" and a couple other gags. I mostly just decided to get silly/creative with it because I couldn't find a whole lot of information regarding the Jack Stauber animation I used.
